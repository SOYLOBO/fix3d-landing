Step 01 — Identify it

What did it do?

You don’t need the right words for it. Point at the one that looks like your problem.

Spaghetti

It let go mid-print and kept extruding. Filament everywhere.

→

Blob of death

Material wrapped the hotend and cooled into a rock.

→

Won’t stick

First layer curls, lifts, or never goes down clean.

→

It shifted

The model slid sideways. Belts, steppers, or something mechanical.

→

Nothing comes out

Clog, grinding extruder, or filament that won’t feed.

→

Won’t turn on

Dead screen, dead board, or power that comes and goes.

Questions

FAQ

What areas do you serve?

North NJ. Drop-off and on-site service are both available, depending on the issue and the machine.

What printers do you work on?

Bambu Lab, FDM/filament printers, resin printers, large-format and specialty machines, and full print farms.

What kind of problems can you fix?

Clogs, bad first layers, extrusion problems, failed prints, belt and mechanical issues, layer shifting, calibration problems, electronics, and preventive maintenance.

What does it cost?

It depends on the machine and what’s actually wrong with it. Send the request through and we’ll tell you what it needs before anything happens.

How long does it take?

That depends on the fault and on parts. We’ll give you a straight answer once we’ve diagnosed it, not before.

I don’t know what’s wrong with it. Is that a problem?

No. Diagnosing it is the job. Pick “I have no idea” on the form and describe what you’re seeing.

How do I get started?

Fill out the repair request with what’s going on and we’ll get back to you to schedule service.
